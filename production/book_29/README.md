## Art QA focus
- Parent/baby relationships must be biologically plausible.
- Young animals must show recognizable juvenile traits without becoming generic cartoons.
- Avoid implying incorrect parental care for species where one parent is normally absent.
- Keep each family grouping visually simple enough for ages 4–8.
- No text, grayscale, hatching, gradients, watermarks, or decorative borders in source art.

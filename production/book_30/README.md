## Art QA focus
- Each shelter or habitat must match the featured animal.
- Keep nests, dens, burrows, lodges, webs, reefs, caves, and other homes visually readable for ages 4–8.
- Do not show animals using homes they do not normally occupy.
- Habitat elements should support the subject without creating clutter or tiny coloring spaces.
- No text, grayscale, hatching, gradients, watermarks, or decorative borders in source art.

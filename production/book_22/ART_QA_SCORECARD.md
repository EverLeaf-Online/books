# Book 22 — Backyard Animals — Art QA Scorecard

Batches: B043–B044
Illustrations: 60
Status: Not Reviewed

## Page-level checks

Every illustration must pass all checks before approval:

- Correct subject and recognizable species/type.
- Child-friendly anatomy appropriate for ages 4–8.
- Bold, smooth black outlines with thinner interior detail lines.
- Pure black line art on white; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, words, signatures, or watermarks.
- Main subject occupies roughly 55–70% of the page.
- Large, simple, enclosed coloring spaces; no clutter or excessive tiny details.
- All anatomy stays inside the 0.50-inch safe area; no accidental cropping.
- Lower caption area stays visually clear for separately typeset animal name and fact.
- Habitat/background limited to about 5–10 large, easy-to-color elements.
- Backyard context is plausible without turning the page into a crowded scene.

## Subject-specific checks

- Backyard birds: bill, wing, tail, feet, and perching posture must be plausible for the listed bird.
- Mammals: paws, ears, tails, body proportions, and coat markings must match the named animal.
- Reptiles/amphibians: limb count, toes, scales/shells, and body shape must be correct.
- Butterflies/moths: paired wings and major pattern regions must remain simple and symmetrical enough for coloring.
- Insects/spiders: correct major body divisions and leg count; avoid extra limbs.
- Millipedes/centipedes/pill bugs: simplify repeated segments without making anatomy misleading.

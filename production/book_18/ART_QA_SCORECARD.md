## Page-level checks

Every illustration must pass all checks before approval:

- Subject clearly reads as the named Australian animal.
- Anatomy is plausible and species-appropriate: correct limb count, feet/paws/claws/flippers/wings, head shape, ears, tail, bill, scales, and body proportions.
- Closely related marsupials remain distinct, especially kangaroos/wallaroos/wallabies, wombats, gliders/possums, quolls, and bandicoots.
- Australian bird silhouettes and bills remain species-appropriate; flightless birds must not be shown flying.
- Marine species retain correct fins/flippers/tails and body plans.
- Reptile/amphibian traits must match the named species; no invented frills, horns, toes, or venom features.
- Main subject occupies roughly **55–70%** of the page.
- Bold smooth black outer contours with simpler thinner interior lines.
- Pure black line art on white; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, texture fills, words, letters, numbers, signatures, or watermarks.
- Large enclosed coloring areas suitable for ages 4–8; avoid tiny repetitive detail.
- No cropped anatomy or key habitat elements at trim.
- Keep all essential art inside the **0.50 in safe area**.
- Lower caption/fact area remains visually clear.
- Habitat uses roughly **5–10 large simple elements** appropriate to the species and does not clutter the page.

# Book 27 — Rainforest Birds — Art QA Scorecard

Batches: **B053 (1–30)** and **B054 (31–60)**

## Per-image gate
- [ ] Correct species/subject and recognizable bird anatomy
- [ ] Bill, feet, wing structure, tail, crest/casque, and body proportions match the named bird
- [ ] Main subject occupies roughly 55–70% of usable drawing area
- [ ] Bold smooth outer contours with simpler thinner interior feather detail
- [ ] Large enclosed coloring spaces; avoid dense feather micro-detail
- [ ] Pure black line art on white; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, or textures
- [ ] Important anatomy and habitat elements remain inside the 0.50-inch safe-content zone
- [ ] Lower page remains clear for separately typeset bird name and fun fact
- [ ] No words, letters, numbers, signatures, logos, watermarks, or decorative borders
- [ ] No duplicated wings/feet, malformed bills, impossible talons, or broken tail feathers
- [ ] Rainforest habitat stays simple: about 5–10 large recognizable elements
- [ ] Output is the canonical 2550×3300 PNG filename from `artwork_manifest.csv`

## Book-specific review
- [ ] Macaws, parrots, cockatoos, hornbills, quetzals, birds-of-paradise, raptors, kingfishers, motmots, toucans/aracaris, tanagers, and hummingbirds remain visually distinguishable
- [ ] Casques, crests, racket tails, long tail streamers, and specialized bills are shown when defining
- [ ] `Bowerbird` is a broad label and receives an appropriate representative design before final approval
- [ ] Hummingbird bills and tail ornaments are plausible and not exaggerated into fantasy shapes

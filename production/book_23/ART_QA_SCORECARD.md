# Book 23 — Desert Animals — Art QA Scorecard

Batches: B045–B046

Status: Not Reviewed

## Page-level checks

Every illustration must pass all checks before approval:

- Correct subject and recognizable animal.
- Child-friendly anatomy appropriate for ages 4–8.
- Bold, smooth black outlines with thinner interior detail lines.
- Pure black line art on white; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, words, signatures, or watermarks.
- Main subject occupies roughly 55–70% of the page.
- Large, simple, enclosed coloring spaces; no clutter or excessive tiny details.
- All anatomy stays inside the 0.50-inch safe area; no accidental cropping.
- Lower caption area stays clear for separately typeset animal name and fact.
- Desert habitat elements remain sparse, large, and easy to color.

## Subject-specific checks

- Desert mammals: ears, feet, tails, horns, humps, and body proportions must fit the listed animal.
- Desert birds: bill, wing, tail, feet, crest/plume, and posture must be plausible.
- Reptiles/amphibians: body shape, limb count, scales, horns, shell, and tail must match the named subject.
- Arachnids/insects: major body divisions and leg count must be correct; simplify detail without adding false anatomy.
- Heat/sand adaptations may be shown visually, but do not invent anatomical features.
- Keep potentially dangerous species child-friendly without turning them into inaccurate cartoon monsters.

# Book 26 — Cute Animals — Art QA Scorecard

Batches: **B051 (1–30)** and **B052 (31–60)**

## Per-image gate
- [ ] Correct subject and recognizable anatomy
- [ ] Friendly ages 4–8 presentation without turning the animal into an inaccurate cartoon
- [ ] Main subject occupies roughly 55–70% of usable drawing area
- [ ] Bold smooth outer contours with simpler thinner interior detail
- [ ] Large enclosed coloring spaces; no tiny clutter
- [ ] Pure black line art on white; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, or textures
- [ ] Important anatomy and habitat elements remain inside the 0.50-inch safe-content zone
- [ ] Lower page remains clear for separately typeset subject name and fun fact
- [ ] No words, letters, numbers, signatures, logos, watermarks, or decorative borders
- [ ] No cropped important anatomy, duplicated limbs, fused features, or malformed paws/wings/fins
- [ ] Simple habitat supports the subject without overwhelming it
- [ ] Output is the canonical 2550×3300 PNG filename from `artwork_manifest.csv`

## Book-specific review
- [ ] `Giant Panda` and `Beluga Whale` labels have been normalized and must match those exact subjects.
- [ ] Remaining broad labels such as Pika, Rabbit, Sloth, Hummingbird, Bluebird, Dwarf Rabbit, and River Otter are checked for a visually appropriate representative form before approval; do not silently invent a species-specific label.
- [ ] Baby/juvenile subjects remain anatomically plausible rather than toy-like
- [ ] Marine, bird, mammal, reptile, amphibian, and insect body plans remain distinct
- [ ] Cute styling does not erase defining markings or proportions

# Book 4 — Jungle Animals — Art QA Scorecard

Use this checklist for every approved coloring page before interior assembly.

## Page-level pass criteria
- Correct subject/species matches `catalog/books/book_04/animals.csv` and the assigned B007/B008 prompt.
- Friendly but anatomically plausible; no duplicate/missing limbs, malformed faces, broken tails, extra eyes, or impossible poses.
- Main subject fills roughly 55–70% of usable page area.
- Portrait 8.5 × 11 composition; preferred master 2550 × 3300 PNG.
- Pure black line art on pure white only.
- Bold smooth outer contours, thinner simple interior lines, large enclosed coloring spaces.
- No grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, texture fills, color, text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures, watermarks, or decorative borders.
- Important anatomy and linework stay inside the 0.50-inch safe-content target.
- Simple jungle habitat only: about 5–10 large recognizable elements; no tiny clutter.
- Lower page remains reasonably clear for separately typeset animal name and fun fact.
- No cropped important anatomy.
- Style matches the rest of Book 4.

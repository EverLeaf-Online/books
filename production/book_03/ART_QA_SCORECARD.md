# Book 3 — Farm Animals Art QA Scorecard

Every illustration must pass all gates before it moves into the approved artwork workspace.

## Required checks per illustration
- Correct species / subject
- Child-friendly anatomy; no malformed limbs, hooves, paws, wings, beaks, horns, tails, shells, fins, or eyes
- One clear main subject
- Main subject fills roughly 55–70% of usable drawing area
- Portrait composition suitable for 8.5 × 11
- Pure black line art on pure white
- No grayscale, shading, gradients, textures, cross-hatching, or color
- Bold smooth outer contours with simpler interior detail
- Large enclosed coloring spaces appropriate for ages 4–8
- No text, letters, numbers, signatures, logos, watermarks, borders, or page numbers in art
- No cropped important anatomy
- Important linework remains inside the 0.50-inch safe-content target
- Simple farm-appropriate background only; avoid clutter
- Clear lower-page area remains available for separately typeset animal name + fact
- No stray objects, duplicated anatomy, or impossible farm equipment
- Consistent visual style with the rest of Book 3

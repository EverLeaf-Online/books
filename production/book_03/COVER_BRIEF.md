# Book 3 Cover Brief — Farm Animals

## Front-cover goal
Bright, friendly MIST° children’s coloring-book cover for ages 4-8. The cover should communicate farm animals immediately at thumbnail size and remain visually distinct from Book 1 and Ocean Animals.

## Required front-cover copy
**Farm Animals Coloring Book for Kids Ages 4-8**

**60 Bold & Easy Farm Friends with Fun Facts**

**Paul Thach**

MIST° branding should be present but secondary to the title.

## Visual direction
Use a cheerful farm scene with a small group of recognizable animals such as a cow, horse, pig, sheep, chicken, and duck. Keep the composition clean rather than crowded. Favor large expressive faces and readable silhouettes. Avoid tiny background details that disappear in Amazon thumbnails.

## Paperback production
- Trim: 8.5 x 11 in
- 128 pages
- B&W white paper interior
- Matte cover
- Spine: 0.288256 in
- Full cover canvas: 17.538256 x 11.250000 in
- 0.125 in cover bleed
- Keep important front/back content at least 0.25 in from outside cover edge
- Reserve barcode area on back cover

## Hardcover production
- Trim: 8.25 x 11 in
- 128 pages
- Matte case laminate
- Do not derive hardcover dimensions from the paperback spine formula
- Retrieve the exact current KDP hardcover calculator/template before final cover assembly
- Keep text/images within KDP hardcover safe zones

## Back-cover copy direction
Short parent-facing copy emphasizing 60 farm-themed coloring pages, bold easy shapes, single-sided pages, and kid-friendly facts. Keep it brief enough for comfortable large type.

## Final cover QA
- [ ] Title readable at thumbnail size
- [ ] Subtitle readable without competing with title
- [ ] Author shown as Paul Thach
- [ ] MIST° shown as imprint/brand, not author
- [ ] No accidental text near trim/fold/spine zones
- [ ] Barcode area clear
- [ ] No transparency/layer issues in final PDF
- [ ] Cover flattened and fonts embedded/outlined as appropriate
- [ ] KDP Previewer shows no blocking cover errors

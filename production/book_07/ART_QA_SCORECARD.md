# Book 07 — Arctic Animals Art QA Scorecard

Use this scorecard for every final coloring page in B013 and B014 before artwork is approved for interior assembly.

## Per-page pass criteria

- [ ] Subject matches the catalog entry and prompt pack.
- [ ] Anatomy is plausible and recognizable for the named animal.
- [ ] Main subject occupies roughly 55–70% of the page.
- [ ] Bold, smooth black outer contours; thinner interior detail lines.
- [ ] Large, simple enclosed coloring spaces suitable for ages 4–8.
- [ ] Pure black line art on a white background.
- [ ] No grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, baked-in color, text, numbers, signatures, watermarks, or decorative border.
- [ ] Full anatomy is safely inside the trim area; no accidental cropping.
- [ ] Important art stays inside the 0.50-inch MIST° safe target.
- [ ] Habitat details are simple and relevant to Arctic/Antarctic context without clutter.
- [ ] Lower caption/fact area remains clear enough for separately typeset text.
- [ ] No malformed limbs, duplicated features, floating elements, merged anatomy, or impossible perspective.
- [ ] Image master is suitable for 8.5 × 11 inch production at the preferred 2550 × 3300 PNG target.
- [ ] Filename matches `artwork_manifest.csv`.

## Page-level checks

Every illustration must pass all checks before approval:

- Subject clearly reads as the named juvenile animal.
- Juvenile proportions are believable and distinct from the adult form where appropriate.
- Anatomy is plausible: correct limb count, joints, paws/hooves/flippers/wings, eyes, ears, tails, beaks, horns, and body proportions.
- No accidental adult-only traits that contradict the juvenile subject.
- Main subject occupies roughly **55–70%** of the page.
- Bold smooth black outer contours; thinner simple interior lines.
- Pure black line art on white; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, texture fills, words, letters, numbers, signatures, or watermarks.
- Large enclosed coloring spaces suitable for ages 4–8; no excessive tiny details.
- No cropped anatomy or important habitat objects at trim edge.
- Keep all essential art inside the **0.50 in safe area**.
- Lower caption/fact area stays visually clear.
- Habitat uses roughly **5–10 large simple elements** and does not overpower the baby animal.
- Expression and pose are child-friendly without making anatomy implausible.
- Species-specific baby terminology and visible traits match the catalog entry.

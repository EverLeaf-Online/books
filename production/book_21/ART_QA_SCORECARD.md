# Book 21 — North American Animals — Art QA Scorecard

Batches: **B041 / B042**

## Page-level pass criteria
- Correct subject identity and recognizable anatomy.
- No extra, missing, merged, or malformed limbs, wings, eyes, ears, tails, digits, horns, antlers, fins, or shells.
- Pure black line art on white only; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, texture fills, words, letters, numbers, signatures, or watermarks.
- Bold smooth outer contours with simpler thinner interior detail.
- Subject occupies roughly 55–70% of the page and remains fully inside the 0.50-inch safe area.
- Large enclosed coloring shapes suitable for ages 4–8; no dense micro-detail or tiny trapped spaces.
- Habitat uses only a few large supporting elements and does not overpower the animal.
- Lower caption/fact area remains visually clear.
- No accidental border, crop, or clipped anatomy.

## North-America-specific checks
- Similar deer-family subjects must retain distinct antler, ear, body, and coat traits.
- Bison, musk ox, bighorn sheep, mountain goat, and pronghorn must not be visually merged into generic horned mammals.
- Bear, wolf, fox, lynx, bobcat, cougar, mustelid, and procyonid silhouettes must remain distinct.
- Bird anatomy must preserve plausible bills, wings, legs, feet, and recognizable species traits.
- Greater Roadrunner must read as a long-legged ground-running bird with long tail and crest; do not substitute a generic cuckoo.
- Reptiles, amphibians, butterfly, and lobster must use correct major body structure and appendage counts.

## Batch approval gate
A batch is approved only when **30/30 images** are marked `Pass` in `artwork_manifest.csv`. Any `Needs Fix` image blocks that batch from interior assembly.

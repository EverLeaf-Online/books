MIST° — Book 16 Baby Animals — Batch B032 — Subjects 31–60

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Original black-and-white children’s coloring-book line art for ages 4–8. Portrait 8.5×11 composition, preferred master 2550×3300 px. One clear baby animal as the main subject, approximately 55–70% of usable drawing area. Bold smooth outer contours, simpler thinner interior lines, large enclosed coloring spaces, generous white space, friendly but anatomically plausible proportions, and 5–10 simple habitat-appropriate background elements. Keep important linework inside the 0.50-inch safe-content target and leave the lower area reasonably open for separately typeset animal name and fun fact. Pure black lines on pure white only. No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, decorative borders, tiny clutter, cropped anatomy, duplicate limbs, or malformed features.

MIST° BOOK 20 — ENDANGERED ANIMALS — B040 — SUBJECTS 31–60

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Original black-and-white children’s coloring-book line art for ages 4–8. Portrait 8.5×11 composition. One clear main animal occupying roughly 55–70% of the usable drawing area. Bold smooth outer contours, thinner simple interior lines, large enclosed coloring spaces, white background, simple habitat with about 5–10 large recognizable elements. Keep important linework inside a 0.50-inch safe-content zone. Leave the lower page reasonably open for separately typeset animal name and fun fact. No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, decorative borders, tiny clutter, cropped important anatomy, duplicated limbs, or malformed features. Friendly and anatomically plausible. Preferred master: 2550×3300 PNG. Do not include conservation-status text or symbols in the art.

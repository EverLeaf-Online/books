MIST° BOOK 27 — RAINFOREST BIRDS — B053 — SUBJECTS 01–30

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Original black-and-white children’s coloring-book line art for ages 4–8. Portrait 8.5×11 composition. One clear main bird occupying roughly 55–70% of the usable drawing area. Bold smooth outer contours, thinner simple interior lines, large enclosed coloring spaces, white background, simple rainforest habitat with about 5–10 large recognizable elements. Keep important linework inside a 0.50-inch safe-content zone. Leave the lower page reasonably open for separately typeset bird name and fun fact. No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, decorative borders, tiny clutter, cropped important anatomy, duplicated wings or feet, or malformed features. Friendly and anatomically plausible. Preferred master: 2550×3300 PNG.

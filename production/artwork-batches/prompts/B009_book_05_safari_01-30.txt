MIST° ANIMAL COLORING ADVENTURES — BOOK 5 / B009
Safari Animals — subjects 01-30

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Portrait 8.5x11 composition for kids ages 4-8. One primary animal, about 55-70% of page area. Bold smooth black contours, slightly thinner interior details, large enclosed coloring spaces, clean white background, 5-10 simple habitat elements maximum, anatomically plausible and child-friendly. Keep important art inside a 0.50-inch safe zone. No text, letters, numbers, captions, watermarks, signatures, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, frames, logos, gore, or cropped anatomy.

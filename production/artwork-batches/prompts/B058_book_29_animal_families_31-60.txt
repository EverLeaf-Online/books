MIST° BOOK 29 — ANIMAL FAMILIES — B058 — SUBJECTS 31–60

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4–8. Portrait 8.5×11 composition. Show one clear parent-and-young family grouping occupying roughly 55–70% of usable drawing area. Bold smooth outer contours, thinner simple interior lines, large enclosed coloring spaces, white background, simple habitat with about 5–10 large recognizable elements. Keep important linework inside a 0.50-inch safe-content zone. Leave lower page reasonably open for separately typeset name and fun fact. No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, decorative borders, tiny clutter, cropped anatomy, duplicated limbs, or malformed features. Warm family interaction, species-appropriate anatomy. Preferred master: 2550×3300 PNG.

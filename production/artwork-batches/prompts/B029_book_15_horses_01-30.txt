MIST° ORIGINAL COLORING BOOK ART BRIEF
Book 15: Horses
Batch B029: subjects 01-30

Shared art direction for every prompt:
- Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4-8.
- Portrait 8.5 x 11 composition; preferred master 2550 x 3300 PNG.
- One clear main horse or pony, friendly and anatomically plausible, filling about 55-70% of usable page area.
- Bold smooth outer contours, simple enclosed shapes, generous white space.
- Show breed-appropriate head profile, mane, neck, body build, legs, feathering, gait, or coat pattern where relevant.
- Use a balanced standing or walking pose; avoid complex foreshortening.
- Add only 5-10 large, simple setting elements such as grass, fence rails, barn silhouette, trail, mountain, or field.
- Keep important linework inside the 0.50-inch safe-content target; leave lower area reasonably open for separately typeset name and fact.
- Pure black line art on pure white only; no color, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, text, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, borders, clutter, cropped anatomy, duplicate limbs, or malformed legs/hooves.

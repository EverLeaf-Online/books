MIST° ANIMAL COLORING ADVENTURES — B005
Book 03: Farm Animals Coloring Book for Kids Ages 4-8
Subjects 01-30

SHARED ART DIRECTION
- Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4-8.
- Portrait 8.5 x 11 composition; one clear main animal filling roughly 55-70% of usable drawing area.
- Bold smooth outer contours, thinner simple interior details, large enclosed coloring spaces, clean white background.
- Friendly but anatomically plausible farm animals; full important anatomy visible.
- 5-10 simple habitat-appropriate farm elements maximum.
- Keep important linework inside the 0.50-inch safe-content target and leave the lower area reasonably open for separately typeset animal name + fact.
- No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, watermarks, logos, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, decorative borders, gore, or cropped anatomy.
- Preferred master: 2550 x 3300 PNG.

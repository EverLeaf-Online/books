MIST° BOOK 30 — ANIMAL HOMES & HABITATS — B059 — SUBJECTS 01–30

GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4–8. Portrait 8.5×11 composition. One clear animal home or habitat as the main scene, with the associated animal visible when appropriate, occupying roughly 55–70% of usable drawing area. Bold smooth contours, thinner simple interior lines, large enclosed coloring spaces, white background, about 5–10 large recognizable scene elements. Keep important linework inside a 0.50-inch safe-content zone. Leave lower page reasonably open for separately typeset name and fun fact. No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, decorative borders, tiny clutter, cropped anatomy, duplicated limbs, or malformed features. Preferred master: 2550×3300 PNG.

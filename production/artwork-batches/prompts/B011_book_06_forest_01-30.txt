MIST° ANIMAL COLORING ADVENTURES — B011
Book 06: Forest Animals Coloring Book for Kids Ages 4-8
Subjects 01-30

SHARED ART DIRECTION
- Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4-8.
- Bold smooth outer contours, simple enclosed shapes, clean white space.
- One clear main animal, friendly but anatomically plausible.
- Simple recognizable woodland setting with 5-10 large habitat elements.
- No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, or decorative borders.
- Portrait 8.5 x 11 composition; keep important linework inside the 0.50-inch safe-content target.
- Keep the lower area reasonably open for separately typeset animal name and fact.
- Preferred master: 2550 x 3300 PNG.

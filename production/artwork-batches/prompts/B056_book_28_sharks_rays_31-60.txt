GLOBAL ART DIRECTION
Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4–8. Portrait 8.5×11 composition. One clear main subject occupying roughly 55–70% of usable drawing area. Bold smooth outer contours, thinner simple interior lines, large enclosed coloring spaces, white background, simple ocean habitat with about 5–10 large recognizable elements. Keep important linework inside a 0.50-inch safe-content zone. Leave lower page reasonably open for separately typeset name and fun fact. No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, decorative borders, tiny clutter, cropped anatomy, duplicated fins, or malformed features. Friendly but anatomically plausible. Preferred master: 2550×3300 PNG.

SHARED ART DIRECTION
- Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4-8.
- Portrait 8.5 x 11; one primary prehistoric animal fills about 55-70% of usable drawing area.
- Bold smooth outer contours, simpler interior detail, large enclosed coloring spaces, clean white background.
- Child-friendly but scientifically plausible body plan for the named animal; no monster exaggeration, gore, or malformed anatomy.
- Use 5-10 large habitat elements maximum; keep the scene simple and uncluttered.
- Keep important linework inside the 0.50-inch safe-content target and leave lower page reasonably open for separately typeset name + fact.
- No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, page numbers, borders, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, or baked-in color.
- Preferred master: 2550 x 3300 PNG.

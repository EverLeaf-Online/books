MIST° ORIGINAL COLORING BOOK ART BRIEF
Book 13: Dogs
Batch B026: subjects 31-60

Shared art direction for every prompt:
- Original black-and-white children's coloring-book line art for ages 4-8.
- Portrait 8.5 x 11 composition; preferred master 2550 x 3300 PNG.
- One clear main subject, friendly and anatomically plausible, filling about 55-70% of usable page area.
- Bold smooth outer contours with simpler interior detail; large enclosed coloring spaces and generous white space.
- Add only 5-10 large, simple, subject-appropriate environment or context elements.
- Keep important linework inside a 0.50-inch safe-content target.
- Keep the lower area reasonably open for the separately typeset subject name and fact.
- Pure black line art on pure white only.
- No words, letters, numbers, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, textures, or decorative borders.
- No clutter, tiny coloring spaces, cropped important anatomy, duplicated body parts, or malformed features.

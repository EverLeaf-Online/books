## Page-level checks

Every illustration must pass all checks before approval:

- Subject is immediately recognizable as the named zoo animal.
- Anatomy is plausible and species-appropriate: correct limb count, feet/paws/hooves/flippers/wings, head shape, ears, tail, horns, bill, and body proportions.
- Closely related subjects remain visually distinct, especially lion/tiger/leopard/jaguar, African/Asian elephant, white/black rhinoceros, camel species, great apes, and seals/sea lions.
- Main subject occupies roughly **55–70%** of the page.
- Bold smooth black outer contours with simpler thinner interior lines.
- Pure black line art on white; no grayscale, shading, hatching, gradients, texture fills, words, letters, numbers, signatures, or watermarks.
- Large enclosed coloring areas suitable for ages 4–8; avoid tiny repetitive detail.
- No cropped anatomy or key habitat elements at trim.
- Keep all essential art inside the **0.50 in safe area**.
- Lower caption/fact area remains visually clear.
- Habitat uses roughly **5–10 large simple elements** and supports the species without clutter.
- No cages, bars, chains, performing tricks, or misleading captivity cues unless specifically required by the brief.
- Pose is calm, readable, and child-friendly while preserving species accuracy.

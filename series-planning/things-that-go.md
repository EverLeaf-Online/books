## Content rules

- one main vehicle per page whenever practical;
- simple environment context: road, station, worksite, runway, farm, harbor, etc.;
- large tires, windows, body panels, and machine parts remain easy to color;
- avoid dense mechanical detail;
- no manufacturer logos, trademarks, racing liveries, or recognizable licensed designs;
- use generic vehicle archetypes rather than branded models;
- facts should explain purpose, motion, parts, jobs, or basic engineering in child-friendly language.

## Art direction

- bold black outlines on white;
- strong readable silhouettes;
- 3/4 view when it helps recognition, but avoid excessive perspective distortion;
- simple ground plane/background;
- friendly, energetic scenes without faces added to vehicles unless a later subseries is intentionally character-based;
- no text or numbers baked into art;
- leave clean lower-page caption area.

This series should inherit the Animal series tooling nearly unchanged:
- 60 illustration briefs per title;
- 2 × 30-image artwork batches;
- exact 128-page assembly map;
- same front-matter structure;
- same paperback/hardcover trim strategy;
- same artwork validator and interior builder with a vehicle-specific art prompt template.

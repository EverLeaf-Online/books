## Content guardrails

- Use current scientific consensus where practical, but keep copy age-appropriate.
- Avoid presenting uncertain reconstructions as hard fact.
- Clearly distinguish dinosaurs from pterosaurs, marine reptiles, and prehistoric mammals.
- Favor species with reasonably established reconstructions and strong silhouettes.
- Avoid gore, graphic predation, or frightening compositions.

## Visual direction

- bold, kid-friendly prehistoric scenes;
- large subjects with simple rock, fern, volcano, shoreline, or forest elements;
- anatomy should reflect current reconstructions where feasible;
- no Jurassic Park-style branding, logos, or trademarked visual designs;
- keep facts outside the generated art.

## First-wave recommendation

Dinosaurs → Baby Dinosaurs → T. rex & Meat-Eating Dinosaurs → Plant-Eating Dinosaurs → Prehistoric Sea Creatures → Ice Age Animals.

# MIST° Things That Go Coloring Adventures — Launch Plan

Status: Planning only while the 30-book Animal Coloring Adventures catalog remains the production priority.

## Core format
- Audience: ages 4–8
- 60 bold and easy coloring pages
- 128 print pages
- Single-sided coloring-page layout with blank reverses
- Short kid-friendly facts
- Paperback 8.5 × 11 in, B&W white paper, no bleed, matte
- Hardcover 8.25 × 11 in, B&W white paper, no bleed, matte
- Publishing order: Kindle title record → Paperback → Hardcover

## Production gate
Do not start full artwork production for this series until the Animal catalog has a repeatable release cadence and several additional books have cleared artwork QA, interior assembly, cover production, Previewer, and proofing.

## Reuse from Animal pipeline
The existing MIST° production system should be reused: 60-image batches, 128-page interior builder, front matter, separate fact captions, artwork validator, KDP preflight, paperback-cover formula, and exact-template hardcover workflow.

## Learning-design rules

- one clear educational concept per page;
- simple copy with short sentences and age-appropriate vocabulary;
- educational text must be separately typeset, never generated inside artwork;
- avoid worksheets that require tiny writing spaces or complex instructions;
- facts and activities should support, not crowd, the coloring experience;
- use inclusive, neutral depictions of children and families;
- health/safety content should remain general and non-diagnostic.

## Art direction

- bold, uncluttered objects/scenes;
- strong correspondence between the visual and the learning concept;
- large open spaces for coloring;
- no decorative text baked into images;
- keep page layouts predictable so parents and children can use the books independently.

## First-wave recommendation

Alphabet → Numbers → Shapes → First Words → Community Helpers → Weather.

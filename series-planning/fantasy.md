## Content and IP guardrails

- Fully original characters and worlds.
- No franchise-inspired costumes, logos, names, silhouettes, or recognizable character designs.
- Avoid copying the visual language of major animation studios or game properties.
- Keep monsters friendly and non-gory.
- Use broad public-domain fantasy archetypes only.

## Art direction

- whimsical, welcoming, simple silhouettes;
- one primary subject or focal scene per page;
- large open coloring areas;
- simple stars, clouds, trees, towers, crystals, flowers, or magical effects as support elements;
- no written spells, logos, labels, or decorative text baked into art;
- keep scenes easy enough for ages 4–8 despite the fantasy theme.

## First-wave recommendation

Fantasy → Dragons → Unicorns → Mermaids → Fairies → Friendly Monsters.

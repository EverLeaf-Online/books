## Content rules

- Separate real science titles from imaginative/fantasy space titles.
- Use child-friendly, current astronomy facts.
- Avoid implying speculative ideas are established facts.
- Generic rocket/spacecraft forms unless using public-domain or clearly generic references.
- Keep technical detail simple enough for ages 4–8.

## Art direction

- large planets, rockets, astronauts, rovers, moons, stars, and simple landscapes;
- black-and-white line art with large coloring spaces;
- avoid dense star fields and tiny control-panel detail;
- keep scientific labels out of generated artwork unless separately typeset later;
- imaginative titles should still use the same bold MIST° visual language.

## First-wave recommendation

Space → Planets → Rockets → Astronauts → Solar System → Moon.

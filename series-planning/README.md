# MIST° Series Planning

This directory contains **planning-only** roadmaps for future MIST° coloring-book series. The active production priority remains the 30-book **MIST° Animal Coloring Adventures** catalog.

## Portfolio order

1. **MIST° Animal Coloring Adventures** — active production; 30-book catalog locked.
2. **MIST° Things That Go Coloring Adventures** — next production series.
3. **MIST° Dinosaur & Prehistoric Adventures** — planned.
4. **MIST° Space Coloring Adventures** — planned.
5. **MIST° Fantasy Coloring Adventures** — planned.
6. **MIST° Learning Coloring Adventures** — planned.

## Stage gates

Future series stay in planning until the Animal catalog has meaningful live inventory. Do not let future-series planning slow artwork, interior, cover, proof, or KDP work for the Animal books.

Recommended release gate for Series 2:
- at least 5 Animal titles live in print;
- repeatable art/interior/cover workflow proven;
- at least 2 physical proofs approved without major layout rework;
- production queue for the next Animal titles remains active.

## Shared production standard

Unless a later series has a good reason to differ:
- target age: 4–8;
- bold/easy single-sided coloring pages;
- 60 coloring pages;
- 128 print pages;
- paperback 8.5 × 11 in, B&W, white paper, no bleed, matte;
- hardcover 8.25 × 11 in, B&W, white paper, no bleed, matte;
- coloring pages on odd/right-hand pages;
- blank reverse after every coloring page;
- 0.50 in internal safe-content target;
- original MIST° artwork only;
- captions/facts typeset separately from artwork;
- publishing order: Kindle title record → Paperback → Hardcover.

## Naming rule

Use a consistent discoverable title pattern:

`[Theme] Coloring Book for Kids Ages 4–8`

Subtitle pattern:

`60 Bold & Easy [Subject] with Fun Facts`

For books where factual copy is not useful (for example fantasy themes), replace “with Fun Facts” with a value-based phrase such as “with Magical Scenes” or “for Creative Fun.”

## Planning rule

Planning files may define titles, positioning, content buckets, keyword directions, art direction, and launch order. They are **not** production-ready books until the active series is intentionally promoted into production and gets its own tracker/build pipeline.

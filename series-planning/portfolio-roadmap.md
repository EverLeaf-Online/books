# MIST° Coloring Portfolio Roadmap

## Objective

Build a recognizable MIST° children’s coloring-book portfolio without allowing future-series planning to slow completion of the active Animal catalog.

## Active series

### 1. MIST° Animal Coloring Adventures

Status: **Active production**

- 30-book catalog locked
- 1,800 coloring illustrations planned
- 3,840 total print pages
- Book 1 print editions in KDP review
- remaining books move through original art → QA → interior → covers → Previewer → proofs → KDP

No new Animal titles beyond Book 30 unless the catalog strategy is deliberately reopened.

## Promotion gates

A planned series becomes an active production series only when:

1. The previous series has a proven release workflow.
2. At least several titles are live and physically proofed.
3. Artwork production capacity exists without delaying the active queue.
4. The new series has a coherent launch wave rather than isolated one-off titles.
5. Metadata, art direction, and production templates are ready before mass illustration work begins.

## Portfolio-wide consistency

Keep these stable across series whenever practical:

- MIST° imprint and visual identity
- age range 4–8
- bold/easy artwork
- single-sided coloring layout
- predictable front matter
- short facts or educational copy where appropriate
- 60 main coloring pages / 128 total print pages
- paperback and hardcover formats
- Kindle title-record-first publishing workflow

## Current priority split

- **80–90% execution effort:** finish Animal series books.
- **10–20% planning effort:** build future-series roadmaps, title architecture, metadata directions, and reusable templates.

Do not generate hundreds of future-series illustrations until the Animal workflow is demonstrably shipping books at a steady cadence.

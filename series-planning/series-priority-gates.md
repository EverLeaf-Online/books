## Promotion gates
A planned series can move from concept planning to detailed planning when:
1. its audience and format fit the MIST° production system;
2. its first 10–20 titles have distinct, non-redundant themes;
3. it has a clear first six-book launch wave.

A series can move from detailed planning to active production when:
1. Animal Coloring Adventures has multiple additional books fully finished beyond Book 1;
2. artwork QA throughput is proven;
3. interior and cover assembly can be repeated without manual redesign;
4. KDP review/proof issues are understood and documented;
5. starting the new series will not delay a nearly finished Animal title.

## Portfolio rule
Finished books outrank new ideas. If a production-ready Animal title is waiting only on artwork, cover work, Previewer correction, or proof approval, that work takes priority over expanding future catalogs.

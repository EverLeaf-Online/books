MIST° ANIMAL COLORING ADVENTURES — ORIGINAL ART DIRECTION v2

AUDIENCE
Kids ages 4-8.

CATALOG
30 books / 1,800 original illustrations / 60 controlled batches.

PAGE
- Portrait 8.5 x 11 master.
- Pure white background.
- Main subject roughly 55-70% of usable drawing area.
- Keep all important linework inside a 0.50-inch safe-content zone.
- Leave clean lower-page room for separately typeset animal name and fun fact.

LINE ART
- Pure black line art only.
- Bold smooth outer contour; slightly thinner interior detail.
- Large enclosed coloring areas.
- No gray, shading, gradients, cross-hatching, texture fills, or solid black backgrounds.
- No words, letters, numbers, page numbers, signatures, logos, borders, or watermarks.

SUBJECT
- Immediately recognizable species/scene.
- Friendly and age-appropriate.
- Anatomically plausible.
- Full body when practical.
- Avoid malformed, duplicated, missing, or cropped anatomy.

BACKGROUND
- Simple habitat-appropriate scene.
- Around 5-10 large supporting elements.
- Never more visually complex than the main subject.
- Avoid tiny leaves, dense grass, heavy fur/feather texture, and clutter.

QA GATE
1. Correct subject.
2. Correct anatomy.
3. Clean black/white line art.
4. No stray objects or accidental fills.
5. Easy coloring spaces for ages 4-8.
6. Safe margins respected.
7. Bottom caption space preserved.
8. No text/watermark.
9. Visual weight consistent with the MIST° series.
10. Approved before interior assembly.
